# Feature 002 - Empty scene

The game will require an empty scene. We will only spectate from a fixed vantage point.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The world exists in rapier3d to provide the basis for future simulation.
- The camera is positioned at the center, laterally, and at a fixed height.
- Movement is out of scope.
- Flat empty scene is rendered.
- High quality, professional grid pattern is rendered on the floor.
- Any configuration that exists uses real units, not magic numbers.
